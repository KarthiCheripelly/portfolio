What I do

  • Instagram — memes about IIT life, "IIT vs IIT" polls and launch posts. Each post is the app's biggest source of new sign-ups.
  • Question of the Day — I write a daily prompt for all IITs, turn some into polls, and let students suggest their own.
  • Push & in-app copy — the 9 PM daily nudge, welcome steps, empty states and buttons, all in plain, friendly English.

  • Growth write-ups — short case studies on retention, rooms and growth, turning data into what we do next.
